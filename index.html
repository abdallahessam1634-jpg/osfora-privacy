Privacy Policy

How Osfora handles photos, discoveries, settings, and purchases.

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Osfora iOS app ("Osfora", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the app.

1. Information processed on your device

Osfora is designed to keep its core discovery information on your device. When you use the app, the following information may be stored locally:

  • Photos you take for a challenge: photos are saved in Osfora's local app storage so you can view your discoveries in your collection.
  • Discovery details: challenge title, category, date, photo filename, and points (XP) associated with a discovery.
  • App settings and progress: challenge start-time preference, reminder preference, onboarding state, completion date, and XP progress.

Osfora uses Apple's Vision framework to analyze a selected challenge photo on the device. The image-classification result is used to help check whether the photo appears to match the challenge.

In the app implementation reviewed for this policy, challenge photos and discovery records are saved locally and are not uploaded to an Osfora-operated server.

2. Camera and permissions

Osfora requests camera access when you choose to take a photo for a discovery. You can deny or later revoke camera access in your iPhone Settings. If camera access is denied, taking a new challenge photo will not work.

The reviewed app code does not request access to your contacts or precise location, and it does not require a user account to use its core features.

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders and grant notification permission, Osfora schedules a local notification on your device before your chosen daily challenge time. You can turn reminders off in the app or manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

4. One-time in-app purchase

Osfora offers a one-time, non-consumable purchase called Osfora Lifetime Unlock. Purchases and restoration are handled through Apple's StoreKit and the App Store. Osfora checks the purchase entitlement provided by Apple to determine whether lifetime access is unlocked.

Payment card and payment account details are handled by Apple, not entered into or stored by Osfora. Apple's handling of App Store account and payment information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and terms.

5. Sharing, advertising, and analytics

The reviewed app implementation does not include an Osfora-operated account service, advertising network, or third-party analytics integration. Osfora does not sell your locally stored photos or discovery records. Apple processes App Store transactions and may process information as described in Apple's own privacy documentation.

6. Data retention and deletion

Photos, discovery records, and preferences remain in the app's local storage until they are removed from the device. Deleting Osfora from your iPhone removes its local app data. If you use iCloud or computer backups, copies of app data may be included or restored according to your device backup settings, which are controlled by Apple.

7. Children's privacy

Osfora does not ask users to create an account or submit personal details to us. The app should be used in accordance with the age rating and guidance shown on its App Store product page. Parents or guardians should supervise a child's use of the camera and remind them to photograph only people or places when they have appropriate permission.

8. Security

Osfora stores discovery photos and related app data in the app's local storage and relies on iOS device security and access controls. Keep your device software up to date and use the device passcode and backup settings that are appropriate for you.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the current version took effect. Please review this page periodically.
